Who am I?

I am consciously blended, and I’m aware of it. All these different sides of me coexist in harmony. Or maybe not.

I see myself as an artist. I am drawn to beauty in all its forms, and I choose to surround myself with it. I don’t compromise on what I believe in.

I’m the main character in my own life, and I’m grateful for everything I get to experience. I take inspiration from what I see, what I feel, and the world around me. That’s how I see and express fashion.
